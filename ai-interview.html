AI interview

Overview

Our AI interviewer will ask you a few questions about your work experience, how you help customers, and the kind of projects you’d like to work on. There are no right or wrong answers. It’s a chance for us to get to know you.

LengthAbout 12 mins
Questions6
FormatVoice + camera

Instructions

The interview works like a video call. Find a quiet place, and check that your camera, microphone and speakers work before you start.

  1. Listen. The interviewer asks one question at a time. The question also appears on screen.
  2. Answer out loud. Speak naturally, the way you would with a person. You can take a moment to think first.
  3. Finish. After the last question, your interview is submitted automatically.
  • Your camera and microphone are recorded for the whole interview
  • You can’t go back to a question once you’ve answered it
  • Keep this tab open. Refreshing or closing it ends the interview
Answer in your own words. The use of AI is prohibited and will result in automatic disqualification.
